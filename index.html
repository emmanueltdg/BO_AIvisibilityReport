🔍 The AI Visibility Landscape

This section analyzes how AI models currently perceive BikesOnline. While the brand excels in Reputation Authority (driven by Trustpilot and ProductReview), there is a significant gap in Topical Authority, which is currently dominated by editorial sites like BikeRadar.

✔ Current Position: Strong Foundation

BikesOnline consistently appears in AI outputs through customer feedback platforms. AI understands that "BikesOnline is a legitimate and well-reviewed place to buy a bike."

Trustpilot ProductReview Reddit

⚠ The Gap: Topical Authority

AI answers are built using two layers. While BikesOnline owns the "Who can be trusted" layer, competitors (Cycling Weekly, Canyon) currently own the "Who explains things best" layer.

⚒ How to Maximise AI Impact

To perform well in AI-generated answers, content should be informative first, not sales-first. Answer specific questions clearly, cover real-world scenarios, and include honest comparisons and trade-offs.

Evolving the Experiment:

Classify queries by source type (Review sites, Editorials, Forums, Retailers) to build highly targeted, complementary content.
